Sure. We **all** heard him that night at the old Black Cat. The tinny rise of the unseen organ calling out to bring into the choir even in the most quiet among us, and the bodies closed in tight and shivered not for warmth, but because they all knew what would come next...

**Surround**. _Surround!_ He calls it as the darkness seeps through the levies, and there is no mercy, no capitulation. The abyss will suffer nothing to speak, and so, the horror you feel goes unnoticed, as your child drowns you expressionlessly.
